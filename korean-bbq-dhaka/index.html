참숯Why charcoal

Charcoal burns hotter and drier than gas. The surface of the meat sears fast and takes on a light smoke, while the inside stays tender. Marinated cuts like galbi caramelise at the edges instead of steaming. It is the way Korean BBQ is done in Seoul, and the way we do it here.
